About ScoreX

ScoreX is built by Unique Labs to make one hard, repetitive job on X manageable: earning the attention of the accounts that matter.

Why we built it

On X, and especially on Crypto Twitter, who follows you matters more than how many do. Influence scores such as TwitterScore measure exactly that. The honest way to raise them is to show up under the right people's posts every day with replies worth answering, and to keep it up for weeks. Done by hand, that is one to two hours a day of watching, writing and tracking. Most people stop after a week.

ScoreX does the routine parts: it watches your list, drafts replies in your voice, follows first, answers replies and records every follow-back. You stay in charge of what is posted.

What we believe

  • You stay in control. Review mode is the default. Nothing posts until you approve it, unless you choose otherwise.
  • Your account, your browser. ScoreX runs in your own logged-in X tab. It never asks for your password, and your data stays on your device.
  • Careful by default. Hard daily limits, human pacing, skip rules, and a full stop on any warning from X.
  • Honest about limits. No tool can promise follow-backs or a score, and automation always carries some risk. We say so plainly.
  • Quality over volume. One reply an author wants to answer beats a hundred that get scrolled past.
