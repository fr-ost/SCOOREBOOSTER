Frequently asked questions

Answers about access, cost, safety and how ScoreX works. Still stuck? See Support or contact us.

What is ScoreX?

ScoreX is a Chrome extension that helps you grow your influencer score on X (Twitter). It watches the accounts you choose, drafts replies in your voice, follows them first, answers their replies and tracks who follows you back.

How do I get access?

Install ScoreX from the Chrome Web Store, then enter the license key from your invite. ScoreX is invite only for now; request an invite if you do not have a key.

Do you need my X password?

No. ScoreX works inside the X tab you are already logged in to. It never asks for, sees or stores your password.

What does it cost to run?

Replies are written with your own OpenAI API key, so you pay OpenAI directly, usually a fraction of a cent per reply. ScoreX shows the real cost per reply and lets you set a monthly budget.

Will it post without me?

Only if you switch to Full auto. In Review mode, which is the default, nothing is posted until you approve it.

Does ScoreX guarantee follow-backs or a higher TwitterScore?

No. It makes consistent, relevant replies easy and shows you what works. Whether an account follows back is always their decision.

Is it safe for my X account?

Any automation carries some risk, and you are responsible for following X's rules. ScoreX is built to reduce that risk: review mode, hard daily limits, human pacing, no duplicate replies, and it stops on X warnings. Read our honest guide to automation safety.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser. Post text goes to OpenAI to write replies, score sites are contacted only if you turn them on, and the license server only checks your key. See the privacy policy.

Which scores does ScoreX track?

TwitterScore, Sorsa, Ethos and Moni. Each is optional and off until you turn it on.

Does it follow people for me?

Yes, if you leave Follow first on. ScoreX follows an account before replying to it and confirms on the profile that X kept the follow. There is a daily follow limit you can lower.

What happens when someone replies to my comment?

ScoreX can write an answer from the whole thread. In Full auto it posts it; in Review mode it waits for your approval. It never replies to insults, and after two answers the conversation is left to you.

Which accounts does it skip?

Accounts that already follow you, accounts that follow very few people for their size (they rarely follow back), and posts that are ads, giveaways, political, or have restricted replies.

Can I use it on more than one X account?

A license key works on one browser installation. ScoreX acts as the account that is logged in to X in that browser and checks it has not changed.

Does it work in browsers other than Chrome?

ScoreX is built and tested for Google Chrome on desktop. Other Chromium browsers may work but are not supported.

How do I stop it?

Press Pause or Stop in the ScoreX dashboard or popup at any time. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on your device.
