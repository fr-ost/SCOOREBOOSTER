Smart Followers on X: Who They Are and How to Earn Them

By Unique Labs · November 14, 2026 · 6 min read

"Smart followers" is the name score sites give to the notable accounts that follow you: founders, investors, researchers, respected builders and established voices in a niche. They are the main input to influence scores like TwitterScore.

What makes a follower "smart"

There is no universal list. Each score site decides which accounts count, usually from their own reputation in the network: who follows them, how long they have been active, and how central they are to the niche. A venture fund, a protocol founder and a well-known analyst all tend to qualify. A brand-new account with a generated avatar does not.

Why they matter more than follower count

  • They move your score. A follow from a high-score account is worth more than thousands of ordinary ones. See how TwitterScore works.
  • They are a trust signal. People look at "followed by" before they decide whether to take you seriously.
  • They compound. When a notable account follows you, their followers start seeing your replies and posts more often, and some of them follow too.

How to see your smart followers

On TwitterScore, your profile page lists the notable accounts that follow you and their scores. The free view shows a few; an account or API plan shows more. Sorsa and Moni have similar lists with different names. Looking at the same list for an account you admire tells you who is paying attention to them.

Use other people's smart followers to find targets

This is the most useful trick in the whole topic. Pick three or four accounts that are where you want to be in a year. Look at who their smart followers are. Those people:

  • are active in your niche,
  • demonstrably follow accounts like the one you want to become,
  • and are often mid-sized, which means they still read their replies.

That list is your target list. ScoreX has a smart follower finder that scans accounts you name, merges the results, and lets you add the best ones as targets in one click.

How to earn a smart follower

  1. Be present under their posts. Reply within the first hour when you can.
  2. Add something they would want to answer. A precise question beats a compliment. Examples are in our reply guide.
  3. Follow them first. It is a small courtesy that makes a follow-back more natural.
  4. Have a profile that explains you. They will look before they follow.
  5. Answer when they answer. A short, good exchange is where most follows come from.
  6. Give it time. Familiarity over a couple of weeks does what one reply cannot.

Who not to chase

Some notable accounts simply do not follow people back. You can usually tell from the follower-to-following ratio: an account following a tiny fraction of its followers has stopped adding new ones. Their posts are still worth replying to for reach, but do not measure yourself by whether they follow you.

Keeping them

Smart followers unfollow accounts that turn into noise. After someone notable follows you, the job changes from getting noticed to being worth the follow: post your own work, keep replies thoughtful, and do not start tagging them in everything.

Frequently asked questions

How many smart followers do I need?

There is no threshold. Each one from a high-score account helps, and a few dozen genuine ones already separate you from most accounts your size.

Are smart followers the same on every score site?

No. TwitterScore, Sorsa and Moni each keep their own list of notable accounts, so the counts differ.

Can I pay for smart followers?

Not honestly. A follow that was bought is not a signal of anything, and services that claim to sell them should be avoided.
