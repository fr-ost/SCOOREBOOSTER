How to Get Follow-Backs From Bigger Accounts on X

By Unique Labs · November 14, 2026 · 7 min read

A follow-back from an account bigger than yours is a small decision made in a few seconds. This guide is about what happens in those seconds, and how to tilt them in your favour.

Why bigger accounts follow someone back

Ask people with large followings why they followed a small account and the answers repeat:

  • "I kept seeing their replies and they were good." Familiarity plus quality.
  • "They asked something I wanted to answer." A conversation started.
  • "Their profile was clearly about the thing I care about." Relevance.
  • "People I respect follow them." Social proof, which is what smart followers are.

None of these are about asking for a follow. Asking is the fastest way not to get one.

Step 1: Choose accounts that can follow back

Start with the follower-to-following ratio. Someone who follows 30% of their follower count is open to new people. Someone at 1% has closed the list. Also check that they post often and that their recent replies show them answering strangers.

Step 2: Follow them, then show up

Follow first. Then reply to their posts over several days. Turn on notifications for a handful of your top targets so you can be early; an early reply is read by the author far more often than the two-hundredth.

Step 3: Make every reply answerable

The reply has one job: give the author a reason to respond or to look at who wrote it. Four reliable shapes:

  • A sharp question: "does this still hold when fees spike?"
  • An implication: "which means the small holders carry the risk first"
  • An honest doubt, with a reason: "fair, but only if liquidity stays after week one"
  • A concrete example from the same space.

Avoid praise, restating the post, and empty reactions. Full examples are in How to Write Replies That Authors Actually Answer.

Step 4: Continue the conversation

When they reply, answer the same day, on the point they made. Keep it short. Concede what is fair. This is where a name becomes a person, and it is the moment most follow-backs happen.

Step 5: Be worth the click

Before following, they open your profile. Make sure it shows what you work on, a pinned post with your best thinking, and recent original posts. A profile that is only replies looks like a growth account.

Step 6: Know when to stop

Give each account a fair window, around two weeks of regular, good replies. If nothing happens, move on without resentment and put that effort into someone new. ScoreX does this automatically: it counts the days from your first comment and retires targets that never follow back.

Common mistakes

  • Replying to everything. Ten strong replies beat a hundred weak ones.
  • The same reply under different posts. People notice, and so does X.
  • Unfollowing right after they follow back. It gets noticed and it burns the relationship.
  • Arguing to get attention. You will get attention and no follow.

A realistic daily routine

  1. Check posts from your target list two or three times a day.
  2. Write 10 to 30 replies you would be happy to sign.
  3. Answer every reply you received.
  4. Once a week, review who followed back and refresh the list.

Frequently asked questions

Should I ask for a follow-back?

No. Asking signals that the follow matters more to you than the conversation. Earn it with useful replies instead.

How long before someone follows back?

It varies widely. Many follow-backs come within the first two weeks of consistent, good replies; some accounts never follow anyone new.

Is it okay to unfollow accounts that do not follow back?

Yes, after a fair window. Do it gradually, and never unfollow someone right after they followed you.
