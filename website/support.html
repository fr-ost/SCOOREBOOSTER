Support

Set-up steps, fixes for common problems, and how to reach us.

Get started in six steps

  1. 1

    Install

    Add ScoreX from the Chrome Web Store and pin it to your toolbar.

  2. 2

    Activate

    Click the ScoreX icon, choose Open dashboard, paste your license key and press Activate.

  3. 3

    Set your handle

    In Settings > General, enter the X handle you are logged in with. ScoreX checks it before every action.

  4. 4

    Add your OpenAI key

    In Settings > Models & costs, paste your OpenAI API key. It is stored only in your browser.

  5. 5

    Add targets

    On Targets, paste usernames or import a CSV. A file with TwitterScores sorts accounts into Tier 1 and Tier 2 for you.

  6. 6

    Start

    Press Start on Today. In Review mode, drafts appear under Review for you to approve, edit or skip.

Troubleshooting

My license key is not accepted

Check for missing characters and that you are online. A key works on one browser installation; if you moved to a new computer, contact us to release it.

ScoreX stopped and shows a safety alert

X showed a captcha, a rate limit or an unusual-activity warning. Open x.com, deal with what X asks, then press Review stop in ScoreX and resume. Consider lower daily limits.

No replies are being drafted

Make sure the engine is Running, your OpenAI key is saved, and your targets show as Commenting. New targets are checked first; accounts that already follow you or follow very few people are skipped by design.

It is not following accounts before replying

Follow first is on by default in Targets > Edit rules. Run the Live X test in Settings > General > Diagnostics; the Follow button row tells you whether ScoreX can see the button on a profile.

A reply failed or was skipped

Open History and click the row: the reason is shown. Common ones are restricted replies, a post that is no longer fresh, or a daily limit.

My replies get very few views

ScoreX shows a reach alert when views drop sharply. Pause for a day or two, lower your limits, and review replies by hand for a while.

The X tab keeps closing or reloading

ScoreX uses one pinned X tab. Leave it open; it is reopened if closed. If X shows no posts in a background tab, switch the worker tab to a separate window in Settings.

How do I move my data to another computer?

Use Settings > General > Backup to export, then import on the other computer. API keys are never exported; add them again.

Check ScoreX yourself

Settings > General > Diagnostics runs a quick check and a live X test that only reads pages. It tells you which part is failing and offers a redacted report you can copy.

Contact support

Email contact@shahriarahmed.net with:

  • your ScoreX version (bottom left of the dashboard),
  • what happened and what you expected,
  • the redacted Diagnostics report, if you can.

Please do not send passwords or API keys. More answers are in the FAQ.
